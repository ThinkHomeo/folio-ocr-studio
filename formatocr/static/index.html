How to use FolioFrom preparing your scans to reviewing, exporting and managing documents

1. Prepare your source pages

Use clear, upright scans with the entire page visible. Original images work better than screenshots with boxes or annotations. Select files in reading order. PDFs and multi-page TIFFs are processed page by page; single-page images become individual pages.

Upload limits: 100 files and 500 MB in total. Supported formats are PNG, JPEG, WEBP, TIFF, JP2 and PDF.

2. Choose the right recognition mode

Text OCR is for books, notes and ordinary documents. Choose the language printed on the page: English, Hindi, or both. Use Automatic layout first; choose Single column or Uniform text block when that matches your source.

Format-aware OCR is for remedy, rubric and printed-grade entries using the TPB 1891 profile. It is not a general table or form reader. Use clean original scans for this mode.

3. Name and number the document

Enter a descriptive title. First page is the printed number of the first uploaded page and may be 0. Last page is optional; leave it blank to calculate it automatically.

Example: Three scans starting at page 12 receive page numbers 12, 13 and 14. These fields label your scans, not a subset of PDF pages. If the expected range does not match the number of scans, all pages are kept and the actual range is shown.

In Format-aware mode, Chapter is optional. Add it now, inherit it from an earlier entry, or assign it later in a batch. Until assigned, exported Chapter values remain blank.

4. Run OCR and review the result

Select your files and click Start OCR. Wait for processing to finish before saving or exporting. Open a page and compare the extracted result with the original scan.

For Text OCR, correct the page text, enter your reviewer name, and choose Save page or Save & next. For Format-aware OCR, select an entry from the scan or review queue, check its remedy, rubric and grade, and choose Save & Next.

Shortcuts: Ctrl+S saves the current review. Ctrl+Enter saves and advances. Alt+Left/Right moves between pages or incomplete entries. Unsaved changes trigger a warning before you leave a review.

5. Resolve missing or uncertain entries

The queue shows 50 entries at a time. Filter by status or review reason, or search for a remedy or rubric. Previous and Skip move through incomplete entries; Next incomplete page finds the next page needing attention.

In Format-aware mode, add a missing entry by drawing its box on the scan, split a merged token, or select entries to merge. Use batch assignment for Chapter or Rubric. Exclude OCR noise only when the scan contains no corresponding entry. Your reviewer name and decision history are retained.

6. Export your document

Draft includes all extracted content. Human reviewed includes saved reviews only. Save your latest corrections before exporting. The preview shows the estimated number of rows and identifies incompatible or empty export selections.

Text OCR exports TXT, Word (.docx) and JSON. Format-aware OCR exports JSON, CSV, SQLite and SQL. SQLite and SQL require Human reviewed scope.

7. Manage completed documents

Use Documents to search, open or rename a saved document. The Reviewed badge means all review decisions are complete; OCR complete means processing has finished and review may still be needed.

Export anything you want to keep, then use Delete beside the document or Delete selected document on a narrow screen. A confirmation identifies the document before its scans, extracted text and review history are permanently removed. Documents still processing cannot be deleted.
